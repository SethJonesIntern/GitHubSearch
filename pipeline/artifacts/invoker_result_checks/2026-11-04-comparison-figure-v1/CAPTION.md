Among 18,961 canonical observed direct invoker methods, PyMethods2Test identifies 262 focal-method correspondences (1.38%), while the independent saved-edge analysis detects returned-value-to-test-check candidates for 359 (1.89%). The sets overlap in 89 methods; 173 occur only in the focal mappings and 270 only in the independent set. 18,429 methods are outside both detected sets. Both approaches are static and subject to false or missing associations. The independent analysis uses an incomplete saved first-edge call forest; correspondence is different from result checking, and neither establishes real-model execution, execution coverage, or semantic correctness.
