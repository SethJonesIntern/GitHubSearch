## What Joern contributes

The source pass follows a call result through local assignments, aliases, attributes, subscripts, and supported transformations into a check. Joern corroboration additionally requires distinct call and check lines in an original shared saved slice, a matching response variable, and a usable slice role. It is stronger evidence than call/check co-occurrence alone.

The saved exports contain slice membership, not complete dependence edges. A matching response slice does not prove a directed dependence path, complete interprocedural flow, check execution, or guard effectiveness. Local-only and unsupported flows remain explicit diagnostics. 22,492 canonical sites have a saved hit; 778 source rows have unsupported scope. Neither a missing slice nor an unrecognized check establishes absence of a guardrail.

## Source review and remaining gaps

The companion source-review CSV records 11 targeted assistant spot checks, not a random precision sample or human validation. Plausible examples include type assertions, invalid result rejection, and Pydantic model validation. An early return for 'No screenshot needed' is an ordinary workflow choice. Two continue cases are Web3 blockchain queries caught by broad invocation patterns, and a third skips empty simulation history. A DashScope provider call also raises a scope/registry audit concern. These concerns are recorded without cutting repositories, changing canonical call counts, or modifying existing research outputs.

Recognized schema-validation routes cover 12 call sites. Parsing-only routes cover 206 sites and are reported separately; parsing can reject malformed responses but is not by itself a semantic correctness check. A zero recognized evaluation-only count does not show that evaluation is absent. LangSmith callback targets, evaluator score propagation, internal structured-output/chain checks, cross-function callers, and guards configured outside the local source flow require further work. The complex LangSmith illustration is therefore not yet comprehensively detected by this pass.

Next: manually validate explicit production candidates and audit the flagged invocation patterns; then extend tracing through evaluation callbacks and caller-return flows before estimating comprehensive guardrail prevalence. Any scope changes need their own evidence and EXCLUSIONS ledger entry.
